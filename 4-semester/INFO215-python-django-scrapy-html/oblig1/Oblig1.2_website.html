Darwin Núñez

Darwin Núñez is a Uruguayan striker who joined Liverpool in the summer of 2022. Known for his pace, strength, and goal-scoring ability, he quickly became a key player for the team. His physical presence and work rate make him a difficult player to defend against, and he has shown incredible potential during his time at Liverpool.
